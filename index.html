sshconnect — Free Online Text to Speech & Image to Voice Studio

Ultra-clean, production-ready web application for real-time Text-to-Voice synthesis, OCR image extraction, and kinetic teleprompting by Vedant Singh.

Features

  • Browser-native Text to Speech with customizable speed, pitch, and volume.
  • Synchronized real-time kinetic word highlighter teleprompter.
  • Client-side optical character recognition (OCR) from images using Tesseract.js.
  • Multimodal AI scene descriptions via Google Gemini Vision.
  • Acoustic harmonic audio spectrum visualizer.
  • Multi-language support across 15+ international locales.
